On the importance of inheritance

أَلْحِقُوا الْفَرَائِضَ بِأَهْلِهَا، فَمَا بَقِيَ فَهْوَ لأَوْلَى رَجُلٍ ذَكَرٍ

۞

“Give the Farāʾiḍ (the shares of the inheritance that are prescribed in the Qurʾān) to those who are entitled to receive it. Then whatever remains, should be given to the closest male relative of the deceased.”

The Prophet ﷺ · narrated by Ibn ʿAbbās · Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 6732
